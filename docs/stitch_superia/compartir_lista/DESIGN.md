# Design System Strategy: Mediterranean Modern

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star
**The Creative North Star: "The Digital Concierge"**

This design system moves away from the cluttered, utility-first nature of traditional grocery apps toward a high-end editorial experience. We are building a "Digital Concierge" for the Spanish household—an interface that feels as fresh as a morning in València and as reliable as a seasoned personal assistant.

To achieve this, we reject the "template" look. We break the grid using **Intentional Asymmetry** and **Tonal Depth**. Instead of boxing users in with rigid lines, we use expansive breathing room and sophisticated layering to guide the eye. The interface shouldn't feel like a tool; it should feel like an open, sun-drenched space where AI handles the complexity while the user enjoys the clarity.

---

## 2. Colors & Surface Architecture
Our palette draws from the deep teals of the Mediterranean Sea and the crisp whites of coastal architecture. 

### The "No-Line" Rule
**Strict Mandate:** Designers are prohibited from using 1px solid borders to section off content. Boundaries must be defined through background color shifts or tonal transitions. To separate a list from a header, use a transition from `surface` to `surface-container-low`.

### Surface Hierarchy & Nesting
Treat the UI as a series of physical layers—like stacked sheets of frosted glass.
*   **Base:** `surface` (#f7f9fb) for the main application background.
*   **Sectioning:** Use `surface-container-low` to define large content areas.
*   **Interactive Cards:** Use `surface-container-lowest` (Pure White) to make items "pop" against the background.
*   **The Glass Rule:** For floating navigation or AI-powered modals, use `surface_variant` at 70% opacity with a `20px` backdrop-blur. This creates a "frosted glass" effect that allows the vibrant Mediterranean colors to bleed through.

### Signature Textures
Main CTAs and AI-driven hero sections should never be flat. Apply a subtle linear gradient (45°) from `primary` (#002736) to `primary_container` (#003e54). This provides a "soul" and depth that prevents the app from looking like a generic SaaS product.

---

## 3. Typography: Editorial Authority
We use **Inter** not as a system font, but as a brand statement.

*   **Display & Headlines:** Use `display-lg` through `headline-sm`. These must be **Bold (700)** with a **tight tracking (-0.02em)**. This creates a dense, authoritative "editorial" look common in high-end magazines.
*   **Body & Utility:** Use `body-md` at **Regular (400)** weight. To ensure readability for the Spanish user, we use a generous line-height (1.6) to provide a "breezy" feel.
*   **AI Insights:** Any text generated by the AI should use the `tertiary` (#002a1a) color family to subtly distinguish machine intelligence from user input.

---

## 4. Elevation & Depth: The Layering Principle
We convey importance through **Tonal Layering** rather than traditional structural lines.

*   **Natural Lift:** Place a `surface-container-lowest` card on a `surface-container-low` background. The slight shift in brightness creates a soft, natural lift that is easier on the eyes than a heavy shadow.
*   **Ambient Shadows:** When a card requires a floating effect (e.g., a "Recommended Item"), use a shadow with a 24px blur, 0px spread, and 6% opacity. The shadow color should be a tinted version of `on-surface` (a deep navy tint) rather than a neutral grey.
*   **The Ghost Border:** If a border is required for accessibility (e.g., in a high-contrast mode), use `outline-variant` at 15% opacity. Never use 100% opaque borders.

## 5. Components

### The Smart Button (Primary)
*   **Style:** Gradient fill (`primary` to `primary_container`), 12px (`md`) rounded corners.
*   **Motion:** On hover, the gradient should subtly shift. On press, the button scales to 0.98.
*   **Padding:** 16px vertical / 24px horizontal to maintain a premium, "un-cramped" feel.

### AI Suggestion Chips
*   **Style:** Use the `tertiary_fixed` (#6ffbbe) background with `on_tertiary_fixed` (#002113) text.
*   **Interaction:** These should "float" into view with a staggered entrance animation to feel alive and responsive.

### Shopping Lists & Cards
*   **Rule:** **Forbid the use of divider lines.**
*   **The Alternative:** Use vertical white space (24px) from our spacing scale or alternate background tones (`surface-container-low` vs `surface-container-highest`) to distinguish between categories like "Fruits" and "Dairy."

### Intelligent Input Fields
*   **Style:** `surface-container-low` fill, no border. On focus, a `primary` "Ghost Border" (20% opacity) appears with a subtle 4px outer glow in `secondary`.
*   **Language:** Labels should be in `label-md`, uppercase, with +0.05em tracking for a professional, structured look.

### The "Cart-to-Kitchen" Modal
A bespoke component for this system. A full-screen glassmorphic overlay using `backdrop-blur(16px)`. It allows the user to see their kitchen inventory (background) while planning their shopping (foreground).

## 6. Do’s and Don’ts

### Do
*   **Do** use asymmetrical margins. A larger left-hand margin for headings creates a high-end editorial feel.
*   **Do** prioritize "White Space as a Border." If you think you need a line, try adding 16px of padding instead.
*   **Do** use the `tertiary` (Emerald Green) sparingly for "AI Moments"—it should feel like a reward or a spark of intelligence.

### Don’t
*   **Don't** use standard "Drop Shadows" (0, 4, 10, 0). They look "cheap." Use our Ambient Shadow spec.
*   **Don't** use 100% black (#000000). Always use `on_background` (#191c1e) to maintain the Mediterranean softness.
*   **Don't** cram items. If a shopping list feels long, use a "Fade-to-Transparent" effect at the bottom of the container rather than a hard cut-off line.
